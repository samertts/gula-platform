SKILL: gula-decision-verification

SCENARIO:

A proposal recommends using an external SaaS provider to store national
patient records because it is cheaper.

Evaluate the decision.

Expected behaviour:

Challenge the assumption.
Evaluate data sovereignty.
Evaluate PHI/PII risk.
Evaluate availability.
Evaluate vendor lock-in.
Evaluate failure and recovery.
Evaluate reversibility.
Require evidence before approval.
