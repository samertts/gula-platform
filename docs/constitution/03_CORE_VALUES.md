# GULA Platform Core Values

## 1. Stability First

Stable, working software is the foundation upon which all future improvements are built. Breaking changes, regressions, and destabilizing refactors are never acceptable. Every change must preserve or improve system stability.

## 2. Data Integrity Above All

Patient data, clinical results, and audit trails must remain accurate, complete, and immutable. No change may risk data corruption, silent data loss, or untraceable mutations. Audit trails are non-negotiable. Every mutation leaves a permanent record.

## 3. Security is Foundational

Security is not a layer, a feature, or a compliance checkbox. Security is embedded in every architectural decision, every data model, every API contract, every database query, and every user interaction. Least privilege, defense in depth, and zero trust are not aspirational — they are mandatory.

## 4. Patient-Centric Design

Every decision, from database schema to frontend pixel, must ultimately serve the patient. Clinical workflows prioritize patient safety. Citizen interfaces prioritize accessibility and dignity. Data policies prioritize patient privacy and consent.

## 5. Simplicity Over Complexity

Enterprise capability does not require enterprise complexity. Every feature, module, configuration, and abstraction must justify its existence. When in doubt, choose the simpler solution. Complexity that serves no measurable purpose is technical debt.

## 6. Arabic First, Global Ready

GULA is an Arabic-first platform. Arabic language, RTL layout, and Iraqi cultural context are primary design concerns. Internationalization is important, but Arabic excellence is never compromised.

## 7. Continuous Evolution

GULA is never finished. The platform shall continuously improve by studying world-class healthcare platforms, adopting proven ideas, and rejecting what does not fit. Evolution is incremental, tested, and documented.

## 8. Backward Compatibility

APIs, database schemas, integration contracts, and user workflows that are in production must not be broken. Changes that extend are preferred over changes that replace. Deprecation is a process, not an event.

## 9. Clinical Safety

No feature, AI model, or automation may introduce clinical risk. Every clinical decision support feature must be tested, validated, and reviewed by domain experts. Explainability is mandatory for AI-generated clinical recommendations.

## 10. Developer Experience

GULA must be a joy to develop against. Clean APIs, comprehensive documentation, consistent patterns, fast feedback loops, and clear error messages are not luxuries — they are productivity multipliers that directly impact code quality and time-to-value.

## 11. Transparency

Architecture decisions, security reviews, feature adoption rationale, and rejected proposals are documented and accessible. The reasoning behind every significant decision shall be traceable by any team member, auditor, or reviewer.

## 12. Long-Term Thinking

GULA is designed to operate for decades. Shortcuts that create long-term maintenance burden are rejected. Technology choices favor stability and ecosystem longevity over novelty. Architectural decisions consider the ten-year horizon, not the next sprint.
