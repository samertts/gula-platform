# GULA Manifesto

## Preamble

We are building a sovereign national healthcare platform for Iraq.

Not as a collection of features. Not as a project with an end date. Not as a technology demonstration. But as a permanent digital infrastructure for the health of the Iraqi people.

This Manifesto states our commitments — to each other, to the clinicians who use our platform, to the patients whose lives depend on it, and to the nation we serve.

## Our Commitments

### To the Patient
**Your health data is yours.** We are its custodians, not its owners. We protect it with the highest standards of security. We make it accessible to you. We never share it without your consent or legal mandate.

### To the Clinician
**We make your work easier, not harder.** Every feature must reduce your cognitive load. Every interface must be intuitive in your language. Every workflow must respect your expertise and clinical judgment. Technology serves you; you do not serve technology.

### To the Laboratory
**You are the diagnostic engine of the nation.** Your results drive clinical decisions, public health policy, and medical research. We give you the most reliable, most accurate, most performant platform ever built for a laboratory.

### To the Nation
**GULA is Iraqi.** Built for Iraq. Owned by Iraq. Sovereign in its data, standards, and operations. We contribute to a healthier Iraq by enabling data-driven healthcare policy, disease surveillance, and national health intelligence.

### To Our Engineers
**Build for the long term.** The platform you build today must serve patients a decade from now. Choose stability over novelty. Choose simplicity over cleverness. Choose maintainability over expedience. Document your decisions. Test your code. Respect the Constitution.

### To the Future
**We prepare the foundation for what comes next.** We do not know what healthcare will look like in 2035. But we know that a platform built on solid architecture, clean data, open standards, and immutable security will be ready for it.

### To the World
**We learn from the best.** We study NHS, Epic, Estonia, Singapore, and every great healthcare platform in the world. We extract their proven ideas. We adapt them to our context. We give credit where it is due. But we remain GULA — a unique, sovereign, continuously improving platform.

## Standing on the Shoulders of Giants

GULA did not invent healthcare technology. We stand on the shoulders of giants:
- The clinical wisdom of NHS, Epic, Cerner, and MEDITECH
- The interoperability vision of HL7, FHIR, IHE, and OpenEHR
- The open-source collaboration of OpenMRS, OpenEMR, DHIS2, OpenELIS, and Bahmni
- The national ambition of Estonia, Finland, Singapore, and Australia
- The global guidance of WHO, NIST, and ISO

We honor their contributions not by copying them, but by learning from them and building something worthy of their example.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to:
- Put patient safety above all else
- Protect data as if it were our own
- Build for stability and longevity
- Continuously improve without breaking what works
- Document our decisions and justify our choices
- Reject unnecessary complexity
- Respect the Constitution
- Hold each other to these standards

**This is GULA.**
