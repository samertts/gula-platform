## 3. Accepted non-blocking notes retained for follow-up

- Documentation drift about client offline support in earlier GULS-020 materials: corrected wording where referenced, but not a gate by itself.
- Legacy `tk`/`syr` identifiers normalize to English pending human choices (tracked in B03).
- Unversioned client cache behavior before this pass: Web cache versioning now exists (`guls-localization-v1`), but activation is not UI-wired (tracked in B05).
- Only short-lived token and fail-closed signature controls were exercised in tests; no production deployment exercised them.
- Locally resolved blockers B09 and B10 remain listed for audit traceability; their PR #308 CI counterparts are still covered by B01.

## 4. Risk statement

The corrections in GULS-021 close the C1/H1–H4/M1–M3/M7–M9 defects and are backed by focused automated evidence. B09 and B10 were also closed locally by the exception-handler split and accessible-label correction. The program still does **not** close CI, native, human-approval, client-integration, state-concurrency, or key-management gates. Consequently it remains **NOT READY — BLOCKED**. No open item in this register is waived by this document.
