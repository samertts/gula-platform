## Preamble

This document establishes the Autonomous Platform Factory for the GULA National
Digital Health Platform. The factory automatically generates, validates, secures,
documents, tests, packages, deploys, monitors, and evolves every future application.

No manual repetitive engineering work shall remain. The engineering platform itself
becomes reusable. The foundation remains frozen.
