## COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS

| Competitor | Type | Strength | Weakness |
|-----------|------|----------|----------|
| OpenMRS | Open Source | Mature, global community | Java-based, LIMS gap |
| OpenELIS | Open Source | LIMS-focused | Outdated UI, limited API |
| SENAITE | Open Source | Modern, Python | Small community, no national features |
| Epic | Commercial | Enterprise-grade | $100M+ cost, Middle East fit |
| Cerner | Commercial | Government contracts | Vendor lock-in, cost |
| LabVantage | Commercial | LIMS leader | Proprietary, expensive |
| **GULA** | **Open Core** | Modern stack, Arabic-first, national | **No revenue (currently)** |

### GULA Differentiators
1. **Arabic-first** — Native Arabic UI, RTL, Arabic clinical AI
2. **National platform** — SNDHS integration, government-ready
3. **Device bridge** — ASTM/HL7 lab instrument integration built-in
4. **Self-healing** — Circuit breakers, auto-repair, degraded modes
5. **AI-native** — Not an add-on, core architecture
6. **Offline-first** — Edge agent for remote labs
7. **Open core** — AGPL ensures community trust

```yaml

  - Inbound: Website, GitHub, content marketing

  - Government: RFP responses, ministry relationships

  - Demo: Personalized platform walkthrough

  - POC: Government/custom requirements proof

  - SaaS: Credit card, monthly billing
  - On-prem: Annual contract, NET30
  - Government: Purchase order, milestone billing

  - Onboarding: Implementation services

  - Success: Quarterly business review
```
