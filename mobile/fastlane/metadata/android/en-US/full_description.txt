GULA Platform transforms how medical laboratories operate in Iraq and the Middle East. Built specifically for Arabic-first healthcare environments, GULA provides a comprehensive laboratory information management system (LIMS) that puts powerful tools in the palm of your hand.

KEY FEATURES:

• Patient Management: Create, search, and manage patient records with support for Iraqi national ID and health ID systems. Master Patient Index ensures deduplication across facilities.

• Order Management: Place and track laboratory orders with real-time status updates. Support for all specimen types, test panels, and priority levels including STAT orders.

• Results Dashboard: View test results as they become available with colour-coded flags for critical values. Includes reference range validation and clinical decision support.

• Barcode & QR Scanning: Integrated barcode and QR code scanning for specimen collection, verification, and result lookup. Supports Code 128, Data Matrix, and QR codes.

• Offline Mode: Full offline capability with automatic synchronization when connectivity is restored. Work uninterrupted even in areas with unreliable internet.

• Biometric Security: Fingerprint and face ID authentication for fast, secure access. Role-based access control compliant with healthcare security requirements.

• Multi-language Support: Full Arabic and English interface with RTL layout optimisation. All medical terminology available in both languages.

• Lab Instruments Integration: Direct integration with Sysmex, Roche, Mindray, and other common analysers via ASTM/HL7 protocol.

• Push Notifications: Real-time alerts for critical results, order status changes, and system updates.

• Audit Trail: Complete action logging with immutable audit chain for regulatory compliance and accreditation.

• Patient Digital Cards: QR-based patient cards with clinical history for rapid identification and data access.

• Sync Engine: Intelligent background synchronisation engine optimised for low-bandwidth environments with conflict resolution.

GULA Platform is HIPAA-compliant and designed to meet Iraqi Ministry of Health data standards. The app uses end-to-end encryption for all patient data both in transit and at rest.

Whether you manage a small private laboratory or a large hospital network, GULA Platform provides the tools you need to deliver accurate results faster.
