GULA Platform is the leading laboratory information management system (LIMS) for medical laboratories across Iraq and the Middle East. Designed for the unique needs of Arabic-first healthcare environments, GULA empowers lab professionals with powerful tools to manage patients, orders, and results efficiently.

With GULA Platform, laboratory staff can:
- Manage patient records with support for Iraqi national ID systems
- Place and track laboratory orders across all specimen types
- View results in real-time with critical value alerts
- Scan barcodes and QR codes for specimen tracking
- Work offline with automatic sync when connectivity returns
- Authenticate securely with biometric login
- Receive push notifications for critical results
- Generate QR-based digital patient cards
- Integrate with major lab instrument manufacturers

GULA Platform uses end-to-end encryption for all patient data and maintains a complete audit trail for regulatory compliance. The app supports both English and Arabic with full RTL layout support.

HIPAA-compliant. Designed to meet Iraqi Ministry of Health standards. Works with Sysmex, Roche, Mindray, and other common analysers.
