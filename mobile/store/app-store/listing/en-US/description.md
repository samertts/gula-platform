GULA Platform is the leading Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS) for medical laboratories across Iraq and the Middle East. Purpose-built for Arabic-first healthcare environments, GULA empowers lab professionals with enterprise-grade tools to manage the complete laboratory workflow.

PATIENT MANAGEMENT
Create, search, and manage comprehensive patient records with Master Patient Index deduplication. Support for Iraqi National ID, SNDHS Health ID, and passport numbers. QR-based digital patient cards enable rapid patient identification and provide instant access to clinical history. All personally identifiable information is encrypted at rest using AES-256.

ORDER MANAGEMENT
Place and track laboratory orders with real-time status updates across the complete lifecycle from collection to reporting. Support for routine, urgent (STAT), and standing orders. Configurable test panels and profiles streamline workflow. Integrated barcode and QR code generation for specimen labelling ensures accurate chain of custody tracking.

RESULTS DASHBOARD
View test results in real-time as they become available. Colour-coded flags immediately identify critical values requiring urgent attention. Reference range validation with age and gender-specific ranges reduces interpretation errors. Delta check detection identifies clinically significant changes. Results can be shared via PDF, printed reports, or HL7/FHIR messaging.

INSTRUMENT INTEGRATION
Direct ASTM/HL7 connectivity with major laboratory analysers including Sysmex, Roche, Mindray, Abbott, Siemens, and Beckman Coulter. Bidirectional communication enables test ordering and automatic result retrieval. Real-time instrument monitoring with downtime alerts.

OFFLINE CAPABILITY
Full offline functionality enables uninterrupted operation in areas with unreliable internet connectivity. Enter patients, create orders, and record results offline with automatic synchronization when connectivity is restored. Intelligent conflict resolution handles concurrent edits. Bandwidth-optimised sync performs well even in low-connectivity environments.

SECURITY
End-to-end encryption for all data in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Biometric authentication with Face ID and Touch ID. Role-based access control with granular permissions. Complete audit logging with tamper-evident chain. HIPAA-compliant and designed for Iraqi Ministry of Health standards.

INTELLIGENT SYNC ENGINE
GULA's advanced synchronization engine is optimised for the connectivity realities of the Middle East. Delta updates minimise data transfer. Automatic background sync ensures data is always current. Queue management prioritises critical updates.

MULTI-LANGUAGE SUPPORT
Full Arabic and English interface with automatic language detection. Complete RTL layout optimization for Arabic. All medical terminology available in both languages. Dates, numbers, and currencies follow locale conventions.

COMPREHENSIVE COVERAGE
From small private laboratories to large hospital networks, GULA scales to meet your needs. Multi-facility support enables laboratory networks to manage operations centrally while maintaining local autonomy.
