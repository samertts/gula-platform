# Privacy Policy for GULA Platform

**Last Updated:** July 15, 2026

## 1. Introduction

GULA Platform ("GULA," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of patients, healthcare providers, and laboratory professionals who use our laboratory information management system (LIMS). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our iOS application and related services.

This policy complies with:
- Iraqi Ministry of Health data protection guidelines
- HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) standards
- Apple App Store guidelines on data collection and privacy
- Applicable data protection regulations in Iraq and the Middle East

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Patient Information
- Full name, date of birth, gender
- National ID number (encrypted), SNDHS Health ID, passport number
- Contact information (phone, email, address)
- Medical history and clinical notes
- Laboratory test orders and results
- Insurance and billing information

### 2.2 Healthcare Provider Information
- Name, professional credentials, license numbers
- Practice/facility information
- Authentication credentials (securely hashed passwords)
- Usage patterns and preferences

### 2.3 Technical Information
- Device identifiers and operating system version
- IP address and network information
- App usage analytics (anonymous, aggregated)
- Crash reports and performance data
- Push notification tokens

### 2.4 Biometric Information
- Face ID and Touch ID templates (stored locally on device using iOS Secure Enclave)
- We never transmit or store biometric data on our servers

## 3. How We Collect Information

- Direct input through the iOS application interface
- Automated collection through app usage analytics
- Integration with laboratory instruments and analysers
- Integration with healthcare facility EHR systems
- Barcode and QR code scanning using device camera
- Offline data entry with later synchronisation

## 4. How We Use Information

### 4.1 Core Platform Operations
- Facilitate laboratory order management and result reporting
- Enable patient identification and record management
- Provide clinical decision support
- Generate digital patient cards and QR credentials
- Synchronise data across devices and platforms

### 4.2 Quality and Safety
- Validate test results against reference ranges
- Flag critical values for immediate attention
- Maintain accurate medical records
- Support clinical audit and quality assurance

### 4.3 Compliance and Legal
- Comply with healthcare regulatory requirements
- Maintain audit trails for accreditation
- Respond to legal requests (as required by law)
- Protect our rights and property

### 4.4 Platform Improvement
- Analyse usage patterns to improve user experience
- Diagnose and fix technical issues
- Develop new features and capabilities

## 5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

### 5.1 We May Share Information With:
- **Healthcare Facilities:** Patient data is shared only with the laboratory and healthcare providers directly involved in patient care
- **Service Providers:** Third-party services that help us operate the Platform under strict data processing agreements
- **Regulatory Authorities:** As required by applicable law or valid legal process
- **Patients:** Patients have the right to access their own data through their healthcare provider

### 5.2 We Will Never:
- Sell patient data to third parties
- Use patient data for advertising or marketing
- Share data with insurance companies without authorisation
- Transfer data to unauthorised third parties
- Use data for purposes other than those stated in this policy

## 6. Data Security

### 6.1 iOS-Specific Safeguards
- Keychain Services for secure credential storage
- iOS Secure Enclave for biometric data protection
- Data Protection API for file-level encryption
- App Transport Security (ATS) enforcing TLS 1.3
- Background fetch and processing with iOS limitations
- Push notification security with APNs

### 6.2 General Safeguards
- End-to-end encryption: All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- National ID encryption using Hardware Security Module key management
- Role-based access control with granular permissions
- Multi-factor authentication support
- Session management and automatic timeout

## 7. Data Retention

- Patient records: Retained in accordance with applicable legal requirements (typically 10 years from last activity)
- Audit logs: Retained for minimum 7 years per healthcare compliance requirements
- Account information: Retained until account closure, then for 90 days pending permanent deletion
- Analytics data: Anonymised and retained for up to 24 months
- Backup data: Retained per backup rotation policy
- Local device cache: Cleared upon logout or uninstall

## 8. Your Data Protection Rights

### 8.1 Patients
- Right to access personal data through your healthcare provider
- Right to request correction of inaccurate data
- Right to request data portability
- Right to lodge complaints with relevant data protection authority

### 8.2 Healthcare Providers
- Right to access, export, and correct data within your authorised scope
- Right to manage user permissions and access within your organisation
- Right to request account deletion

## 9. International Data Transfers

GULA Platform primarily stores data within the region of operation. When data is transferred across borders, we ensure adequate safeguards are in place through standard contractual clauses and data processing agreements.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not intended for use by children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we become aware of such collection, we will take steps to delete the information promptly.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We will notify users of material changes to this Privacy Policy through in-app notifications, email, and website announcements.
