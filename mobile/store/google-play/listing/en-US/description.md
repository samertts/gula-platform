# GULA Platform - Laboratory Information Management System

## Transform Your Laboratory Operations

GULA Platform is a comprehensive, cloud-based Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS) purpose-built for medical laboratories in Iraq and the Middle East. Designed with an Arabic-first approach, GULA empowers laboratory professionals to manage their entire workflow—from patient registration and order placement to result reporting and instrument integration—all from a single, intuitive platform.

## Why GULA?

**Built for the Region**
Developed specifically for Iraqi and Middle Eastern healthcare environments, GULA supports local identification systems, Arabic language throughout, and regional regulatory requirements including Iraqi Ministry of Health data standards.

**Complete Workflow Coverage**
From the moment a patient arrives at the lab to the delivery of final results, GULA tracks every step of the laboratory workflow. Sample collection, transport tracking, accessioning, analysis, verification, and reporting are all managed within the platform.

**Always Available**
Our intelligent sync engine allows uninterrupted operation even in areas with unreliable internet connectivity. Data synchronizes automatically when connectivity is restored, with built-in conflict resolution.

## Core Capabilities

### Patient Management
- Comprehensive patient registry with Master Patient Index (MPI) for deduplication
- Support for Iraqi National ID, SNDHS Health ID, and passport numbers
- QR-based digital patient cards with clinical history
- Encrypted storage of all personally identifiable information
- Family and group linking for multi-patient management

### Order Management
- Create and track laboratory orders with real-time status updates
- Support for routine, urgent (STAT), and standing orders
- Configurable test panels and profiles
- Barcode and QR code generation for specimen labelling
- Collection confirmation and chain of custody tracking
- Integration with electronic health record (EHR) systems

### Results Management
- Real-time result entry and validation
- Automatic critical value flagging and alerting
- Reference range validation with age and gender-specific ranges
- Delta check detection for significant changes
- Clinical decision support integration
- PDF and printable result reports
- HL7/FHIR result messaging

### Instrument Integration
- Direct ASTM/HL7 connectivity with major analysers
- Support for Sysmex, Roche, Mindray, Abbott, Siemens, Beckman Coulter, and more
- Bidirectional communication for test ordering and result retrieval
- Automatic validation and verification workflows
- Instrument downtime monitoring and alerting

### Financial Management
- Test pricing and billing integration
- Insurance claim support
- Revenue tracking and reporting
- Discount and package management

### Quality & Compliance
- Built-in quality control tracking
- IQC (Internal Quality Control) and EQA (External Quality Assessment) management
- Audit trail with immutable logging
- HIPAA-compliant security controls
- Iraqi Ministry of Health readiness

## Security & Privacy

- End-to-end encryption for all patient data in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- Biometric authentication (fingerprint, face ID)
- Role-based access control with granular permissions
- Complete audit logging with tamper-evident chain
- Encrypted national ID storage with separate key management
- Session timeout and device management
- SOC 2 compliant infrastructure

## Offline Capabilities

GULA's advanced sync engine enables full functionality without an internet connection:
- View and edit patient records offline
- Create and manage orders offline
- Record results with time-stamped offline entries
- Scan barcodes and QR codes offline
- Automatic synchronization when connection is restored
- Intelligent conflict resolution for concurrent edits
- Bandwidth-optimised sync for low-connectivity environments
